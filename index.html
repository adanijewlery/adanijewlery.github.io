Speak with ADANI ↗
How do I enquire about a piece?

Open a photograph and choose “Enquire about this piece”. Your WhatsApp draft includes the piece’s name, ready for your questions.

Can we discuss a personal design?

Choose a piece, explore a style and gold colour, then send your design brief. The preview is AI-generated inspiration; final materials, dimensions and price are confirmed personally.

What should I look for in a diamond?

Look at cut, colour, clarity and carat weight together. Brilliant cuts show bright and dark facet patterns with small flashes of colour; step cuts show long, clear reflections. The stone’s dimensions and setting also shape its appearance.

Can I see the diamond’s grading report?

Ask ADANI which report accompanies your selected stone and confirm its origin and specifications. A concept image does not establish a grade or certification.

Explore independent grading guidance: GIA · IGI · CGL · SGL.

What about availability and delivery?

Ask the house about current availability, delivery options and timing for your selected piece before placing an order.
