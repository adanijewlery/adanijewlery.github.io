Our Heritage

Our Story

Some names are created to become brands.

Others carry the legacy of generations.

ADANI is one of them.

For five generations, the Adani family has devoted itself to the art of goldsmithing and fine jewelry.

A family story spanning continents and cultures — from India and Israel to Singapore and Italy, and across to Miami and Los Angeles.

Throughout the generations, knowledge, tradition, and a passion for creation have been passed down, along with an enduring commitment to precision, excellence, creativity, and the art of fine craftsmanship.

This is more than our history.

It is our identity.
